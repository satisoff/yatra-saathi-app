Install

Get it on your phone

Yatra Saathi isn't on the Play Store yet. You install it straight from this page, which takes about a minute.

  1. Download the app

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download. The file is about 81 MB.

  2. Open the file

    Tap the download when it finishes. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or Files app. Allow it once.

  3. If Play Protect asks

    Android checks apps that don't come from the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

  4. Updating later

    Install the newer file the same way. It replaces the old app and keeps all your trips.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. The app runs on free, open data and has no paid features. You only pay for your actual trip.

How accurate are prices and train times?

Prices and fares are estimates and are marked that way. Train times come from a 2016 timetable, so check IRCTC or NTES before you book. When you find the real price or hours, correct it in the app and your plan updates.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Yatra Saathi is Android only for now, on Android 7.0 or newer.

What does it do with my location?

It uses your location to plan from where you are, and during an active trip to suggest nearby places that fit your plan and budget. Android asks you first, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.

Why does Android warn me when I install it?

Android shows a warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store. Yatra Saathi doesn't ask to read your messages or notifications. You add payments by pasting the SMS yourself.
